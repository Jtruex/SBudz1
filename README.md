Main Idea:
General Thesis: Finding cybersecurity vulnerabilities at smaller companies. Large companies have large teams working on their security. Creating a system that could find vulnerabilities for small businesses will be a great diverse project for the Stud Budz. Creating a system of stress tests for small companies could lead to charging for services.
A larger vulnerability that I think is overlooked is newer apartment buildings and their Wi-Fi and door access.

-Finding vulnerabilities in smaller companies and establishments with the managements' consent. Free of charge to stress test our programming
(JT: I have 12 or so local spots in Indiana that would be open to something like this)
-Work on creating a set of things that can make a smaller establishment vulnerable
  -Wifi Vulnerability
  -POS Attacks
  -DDOS Attacks
  -Social Atacks
  -SQL Attacks
